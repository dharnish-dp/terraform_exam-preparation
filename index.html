Exam day, step by step

  1. Book the exam through HashiCorp's certification portal and pick a time slot.
  2. Check your setup a day before: webcam, microphone, stable internet, supported browser, the proctor's secure-browser tool.
  3. Prepare the room: quiet, clear desk, no phones, notes or extra monitors. Have a government photo ID ready.
  4. Check in 15 minutes early: ID photo, face photo, and a webcam sweep of the room.
  5. Take the exam: one question at a time, with a timer and the ability to flag and revisit questions.
  6. Submit: you see pass/fail straight away, then get an email with details.

How these practice papers work

  • Each paper has 30 questions over all 8 exam domains, and a 35 minute timer (slightly more than the real per-question pace).
  • You get a question grid, a flag for review button, and an automatic submit when the timer ends.
  • After you submit you see PASS / FAIL (70% mark), your score per domain and a full review with explanations.
  • Your progress is saved in your own browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Aim for 80% or more on all three before booking the real exam.

These are original practice questions, not real exam questions. Answers are stored in the page source, so don't peek: it defeats the purpose.
